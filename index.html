Frequently Asked Questions

Are all perfumes 100% authentic?

Every product is sourced from authorized distributors and comes with original packaging, batch codes, and manufacturer seals. We guarantee 100% authenticity on every item — and we're happy to share purchase receipts on request.

How does ordering via WhatsApp work?

Tap the "Order Now" button on any product or bundle. It opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to our team. We'll confirm availability, arrange payment, and schedule delivery — all through chat. Most orders are confirmed within 30 minutes.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across all seven emirates. Dubai and Abu Dhabi orders typically arrive within 24 hours. Other emirates within 2-3 business days. Express same-day delivery is available in Dubai.

Is gift wrapping included?

Yes! Every order comes beautifully gift-wrapped at no extra charge. Bundles include premium packaging with a handwritten card. Just tell us the recipient's name and your message when ordering.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, cash on delivery (COD), and card payments via payment link. Our team will discuss the best option for you on WhatsApp.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Due to the nature of perfume products, we cannot accept returns on opened items. If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, we'll replace it immediately — contact us within 24 hours of delivery.

Will my order arrive before Valentine's Day?

Orders placed by February 12th are guaranteed to arrive by Valentine's Day. We recommend ordering early as gift sets sell out fast. Express same-day delivery is available in Dubai for last-minute orders.
